### The meaning of the fields:

Visible: Whenever to show the progress info,
weekKey: the calendar week,
goalSeconds: Weeekly goal but in **seconds**
rentWeek: this field tells if the rent week is active and users need to pay rent.
week.totalSeconds: Total tracked coding time
week.aiSeconds: Coding time when AI was used
week.aiCreditedSeconds: AI-associated time that will be count forward the progress(usually 3x slower)
week.creditedSeconds: Coding time count toward the weekly goal in **seconds** without AI.
unclaimed: Separate time totals classified as unclaimed
computedAt: Timestamp when the data was calculated
